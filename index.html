Assumptions & formulas

1) Join transaction and address rows by UTC date; exclude the current UTC date and use the newest matched complete dates. 2) Select 7, 14, or 21 days and compare with the immediately preceding equal-length window. 3) Daily means are the sum of daily values divided by days observed; active accounts are not deduplicated across days. 4) Falsification tolerance: a decline greater than 20% fails a test; this is a tunable prototype guardrail, not an external benchmark. 5) Fee and volume are displayed in the units returned by NearBlocks v3; fees are not revenue, and volume is not monetization.

Rebuild this view
  1. Fetch the three v3 URLs above (the first two request the latest 50 daily observations; the transaction query is a 25-row example sample).
  2. Join daily transaction and address series on date; sort ascending and take the latest selected window plus the previous same-length window.
  3. Recompute average transactions, active accounts, transaction fees, and transfer volume per day; report percentage change against the matched period.
  4. For the busy-day test, remove the highest-transaction day and compare the remaining daily mean with the prior-window daily mean.
  5. For the signer test, group only the 25-row sample by signer_account_id; removing that signer changes the sample, never the network-wide baseline.
  6. Apply the selected activity haircut arithmetically and label every output “hypothetical.” No scenario result is written back to chain data.

Limits to the claim

Active accounts do not equal people or retention. A 25-transaction signer sample is deliberately not extrapolated to the whole network. Transaction fees are not protocol revenue, and transfer volume is a flow proxy. NearBlocks is an indexer; source records and interpretation should be independently checked before investment conclusions.
