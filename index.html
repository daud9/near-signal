Interpretation guardrails

Fees are an observable cost flow

NearBlocks reports indexed fees in Ⓝ. This dashboard does not treat them as protocol revenue or infer price appreciation.

More transactions do not guarantee more value capture

Low-cost activity can grow quickly while fee totals remain flat. The matched comparison shows both separately.

Indexed volume is not capital flow

It can include gross movements and repeated transfers. No net inflow, capital retention or investment conclusion is calculated from it.

Methodology & Data Provenance

Every displayed statistic is tied to a public endpoint, calculation rule, freshness date and caveat.

Metric definitions

Matched daily observation

Join NearBlocks transaction and address statistics on the same UTC date. Exclude the current UTC date and exclude days missing either essential transaction or active-account values. Keep source lag visible.

Window change

Compare the mean of the selected N complete matched days with the preceding N days. Require exact daily continuity and both full windows; otherwise report no matched-period change.

Activity per account

Mean daily transactions divided by mean daily active accounts. This is a derived frequency proxy; it does not identify unique people or retention cohorts.

Sample signer HHI

For the newest returned transaction rows, compute 10,000 × Σ(share of rows by signer)². The recent sample is never extrapolated to all NEAR activity.

Hypothesis correlation

Pearson r between daily transaction counts and active-account counts over the two matched windows. No p-value, causal inference or prediction is asserted. Verdict thresholds are disclosed next to the result.

Missing data

No synthetic fallback is loaded. Null or unavailable data stays unavailable, and the dashboard withholds NEHS when critical dimensions are missing.

NEHS scoring policy

The overall 0–100 score is withheld in this MVP because available evidence is insufficient. Equal category weights below are provisional design choices—not empirical weights or a score currently applied to NEAR.
