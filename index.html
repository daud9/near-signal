Ecosystem Risk Radar

Live, source-backed findings only. Severity describes potential impact; confidence describes evidence quality. The adjustable threshold affects only the market and 30-day ecosystem TVL signals.

20%
Risk rulebook & live coverage

Stablecoin depeg: compare the provider’s price with $1 only for assets classified as USD-pegged and with reported NEAR-chain circulation. Critical at ≥5% deviation on one live check; high at ≥3% on two checks; moderate at ≥1% on two checks within 72 hours. The stablecoin response has no provider timestamp/confidence field, so cards show the browser check time and do not claim redemption or reserves.

Aggregate stablecoin supply: compare DefiLlama’s totalCirculating.peggedUSD values over eight consecutive complete daily observations. A decline of at least 10% across seven days is a high investigation watch; missing dates are not filled. Provider coverage changes, reclassification or migrations can explain the series, so it is not proof of withdrawals, redemption, reserves or solvency stress.

NEAR protocol liquidity: the app selects up to eight non-CEX protocols with reported NEAR-chain TVL ≥$100,000, then reads dated chainTvls.Near.tvl history. High at ≥20% decline over a dated observation window near one day or ≥30% over seven days; critical at ≥40% or ≥50%, respectively. No interpolation; USD TVL decline is not proof of withdrawals or an exploit.

Validator performance: the finalized validator response is checked for prior-epoch kickouts and current slashing flags. Kickouts are direct, completed-epoch protocol records but describe affected validators, not necessarily network failure; slashing is shown separately.

APY coverage: there is no universal, free keyless NEAR yield feed in the connected sources; DefiLlama’s public yield routes stopped working on 13 November 2026. The radar does not invent or infer APR/APY alerts from token price or TVL. A defensible per-protocol yield check needs verified contract methods and reward/fee accounting.

Evidence: cards include the observed values, threshold, source links, and a reproducible request. Stale threshold crossings remain visible as historical context but are not counted as active findings. A missing feed is never treated as a zero-risk result. See the method and limitations and the seven-domain risk research.

Risk boundary: a threshold crossing is a prompt for investigation, not proof of failure, manipulation or a security event. One RPC provider’s view is not a global network health assessment.

How to interpret TVL

TVL is a reported asset stock

It records the dollar value held in covered DeFi protocols at a daily point. Token-price movements and provider coverage can change the USD value.

Compare matched dates

The TVL chart uses this source's own dated history; missing dates are left out, not interpolated.

Do not read it as a flow

A TVL change is not deposits, bridge direction, net investment or retained capital.
