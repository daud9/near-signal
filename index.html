Methodology & Data Provenance

Each observation has a source, fetch time or provider time, cadence and scope limitation.

Live metric definitions

Finalized block freshness

Read NEAR RPC block with finality: "final". Age is browser time minus the returned nanosecond block timestamp. It describes the queried endpoint only.

Latest-block gas cost estimate

Sum gas_used across the returned chunks, multiply by that block’s gas_price, and divide by 1024 yoctoNEAR per Ⓝ. It is a block-level gas-cost estimate, not protocol revenue or all transaction charges.

Validator-set concentration

Use the RPC validators([null]) current active set. Top-10 share is the sum of those ten validators’ reported stake divided by stake in the returned set. It is not the full delegation distribution and has no historical trend until multiple epochs are collected.

Market snapshot

CoinGecko supplies aggregated NEAR price, market cap, gross 24-hour market volume, price change and provider timestamp. DefiLlama spot is shown only as an independent timestamped price cross-check. Neither is an on-chain capital-flow measure.

TVL history and matched dates

DefiLlama TVL is a daily USD stock. The app joins it to CoinGecko daily market observations by exact UTC date and excludes the current partial UTC day. It does not fill missing dates.

Transactions per sampled finalized block

Read one finalized block, then call chunk by that block height and each returned shard ID. Verify every returned chunk hash, shard ID and inclusion height; sum the lengths of all shard transactions arrays. If any shard is missing or mismatched, withhold the total. Sample no more than every five minutes. The app stores only the count, chunk hashes, shard counts and timestamps—not transaction identities or contents.

Hypothesis test

Calculate daily percentage changes and Pearson correlation on the selected aligned series. Require at least five matched changes. The ±0.30 decision boundary is a visible product rule, not a validated significance threshold; no p-value, causal or predictive claim is made.

Missing evidence

No account-history key, JWT or wallet data is configured. The app uses no synthetic fallback. Daily account cohorts, returning-user retention, verified people, reconciled net flows, application concentration, dependency mapping and an overall NEHS score remain unmeasured.
