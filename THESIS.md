# NEAR SIGNAL — Investment Thesis

## Claim

NEAR's long-term investment case depends on sustained, broad on-chain activity and cross-chain settlement translating into durable protocol-level value capture. The [official Investment Thesis prompt](https://github.com/nearai/near-hackathon-prompt-examples/blob/main/tracks/02-thesis.md) suggests testing whether NEAR Intents cross-chain volume is a better leading indicator of real network usage than token price.

**Current conclusion: not established; the available market evidence argues for caution.** Connected sources show a dated decline in price, TVL and gross market volume, but the data required to test organic activity, Intents settlement and protocol-wide value capture are not connected. This is an evidence assessment, not investment advice.

## Evidence that cuts against a bullish growth reading

The latest evidence snapshot was generated **14 November 2026 at 22:42 UTC** from **15/15 successful connected feeds**. It contains 31 matched daily market/TVL dates from **14 October to 13 November**. Across those dates, the NEAR price endpoint fell **32.11%** (about $5.04 to $3.42), NEAR DeFi TVL fell **34.53%** (about $243.1 million to $159.2 million), and gross market-volume endpoint fell **63.62%** (about $1.12 billion to $408.4 million). These are provider-reported USD observations; they are not net investment, on-chain activity, protocol revenue, or user counts.

The current default challenge uses 14 matched daily changes from **31 October to 13 November**. Pearson **r = 0.9796**; **11** pairs moved in the same direction and **3** in opposite directions (**78.57%** same-sign agreement, with no zero-change ties). The leave-one-day-out correlations range from **0.9719 to 0.9864**; omitting **3 November** changes `r` the most, by about **0.0076**. These are descriptive diagnostics over this small selected window, not a confidence interval or statistical test.

“Supported by available evidence” means the full-window correlation clears the declared positive co-movement threshold. Both series' levels fell over the broader sample; the result does **not** indicate a bullish trend, growth, causal influence, statistical significance, or a forecast.

## Evidence needed to test the broader thesis is unavailable

Public chain and [NEAR Intents Explorer documentation](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/explorer/introduction) describe data that could inform the missing tests. Documentation and endpoint availability do not establish that the dashboard has complete, authorized historical data or that the missing metrics are increasing. The Intents Explorer covers historical 1Click swaps only and requires partner access.

## What is unknown or cuts against the hypothesis

- No daily active signer-account series, daily transaction trend, contract-function-call history, or account-retention cohort is connected. A signer account is not a person, and activity counts do not prove organic or non-incentivized usage.
- No NEAR Intents swap-volume data is connected. The Explorer covers 1Click swaps only, not every Intents route, and a status record alone does not prove final destination delivery.
- The live gas estimate is a recent block-level proxy, not a reconciled fee ledger. The app now shows the protocol-version-matched `burnt_gas_reward` rule and DefiLlama-indexed daily fee context, but neither identifies all receipt-level refunds, exact signer debits, realized beneficiaries, issuance, treasury flows or durable tokenholder value capture. The RPC's prior-epoch validator reward map is also separate from transaction-fee revenue.
- TVL is a USD-denominated asset stock and can move with token prices or provider coverage. DefiLlama DEX volume is turnover, not deposits or directional net flow; GeckoTerminal page 1 is only a bounded pool-market snapshot, not every NEAR pool or a chain-wide liquidity total. Gross exchange volume is also turnover, not chain transactions or directional net capital flow.
- No multi-provider uptime history, multi-epoch validator trend, complete app/contract inventory or bridge/security dependency history is available.

Missing data is neither support nor disproof. It leaves the working hypothesis untested.

## What would change my mind

I would move toward supporting the long-term thesis if a reproducible period of at least 90 days showed sustained or broadening daily signer activity and contract calls, meaningful returning-account cohorts under a published definition, and rising finalized Intents completions reconciled across deposits, `intents.near` settlement and destination delivery. A separate protocol-version-aware ledger would need to reconcile `tokens_burnt`, refund receipts, issuance, validator rewards and treasury flows before I would claim durable value capture.

I would move against the thesis if those validated series showed persistent activity contraction, usage concentrated in a shrinking set of accounts/contracts, a large share of Intents records failing or not completing at destination, or fee burns remaining small relative to issuance and rewards throughout the measured period. Definitions, date ranges, thresholds and exclusions must be published before evaluating the result.

## Methodology and connected data sources

The app reads [FastNEAR's public mainnet RPC](https://free.rpc.fastnear.com) for finalized blocks/chunks, block-specific protocol config, node status and current/next validator data; [FastNEAR Neardata](https://docs.fastnear.com/neardata) for exact-height block samples; CoinGecko for price and market context; and DefiLlama for independent spot price, [daily NEAR TVL](https://api.llama.fi/v2/historicalChainTvl/near), a filtered protocol index with up to eight detailed [protocol histories](https://api.llama.fi/protocols), [stablecoin price/NEAR circulation](https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoins?includePrices=true), [dated aggregate NEAR stablecoin circulation](https://stablecoins.llama.fi/stablecoincharts/Near), [NEAR DEX volume](https://api.llama.fi/overview/dexs/Near) and [indexed chain fees](https://api.llama.fi/overview/fees/Near). [GeckoTerminal](https://api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2/networks/near/pools?page=1) adds only a page-one pool snapshot. Each observation retains its provider or local check time and response status. The [source inventory](docs/data-sources.md) lists cadence and scope. Inside the Thesis Challenge Engine, exact chart requests, observation times, paired changes, directional/leave-one-day-out diagnostics, calculation steps and limitations appear beside the result and in its JSON export; risk cards disclose their own observed values, thresholds and reproduction queries.

Daily market and TVL series are matched on exact UTC dates, exclude the partial current day and never fill missing dates. A 30-day endpoint change requires 31 consecutive dates. The descriptive test first calculates percentage changes between exactly consecutive daily observations, then pairs changes on their UTC end date and requires the complete selected window of 7, 14 or 30 consecutive matched changes. Pearson `r` describes co-movement only; ±0.30 is a product rule, not a significance threshold, and no p-value or causal estimate is calculated. Direction agreement excludes zero-change ties; leave-one-day-out ranges are descriptive influence checks, not confidence intervals or robustness proofs. Each finalized-block transaction sample requires every newly included shard chunk to match the finalized block's hash, shard ID and inclusion height; earlier-height references contribute zero. Stale threshold crossings remain visible as context but are excluded from active-finding counts. Risk severity and evidence confidence are separate. Stablecoin peg, aggregate supply, protocol-TVL and validator rules have specialized thresholds; the configurable decline threshold still applies only to market/30-day ecosystem TVL signals. Calculations are deterministic and tested; no source gaps are filled. See the [full definitions](docs/methodology.md).

## Intents API access

The Explorer is read-only historical **1Click Swap** coverage at `https://explorer.near-intents.org/api/v0`. Its docs require a partner JWT from the [Partner Dashboard](https://partners.near-intents.org/) in `Authorization: Bearer <JWT>`, use cursor-based pagination and limit requests to one every five seconds per partner. No token is configured and no request is made. A future integration must keep the JWT server-side and reconcile records with finalized settlement and destination-chain/bridge evidence.

## Rebuild and track status

Run `npm test`, `npm run check`, then `npm run rebuild`. The rebuild refreshes 15 connected public-source feeds and writes `data/latest-evidence.json` with market/TVL/block and risk inputs, the independent long TVL series, raw dated stablecoin supply rows, DEX and indexed-fee rows, the bounded pool snapshot, block-matched protocol config, source statuses/times, exact Thesis paired changes and diagnostics, and query recipes. `npm run replay` reconstructs charts from an existing evidence file without network access. Each displayed chart discloses its source request or calculation, derivation and plain-language meaning.

This rebuild covers currently connected inputs, not every intended track metric: daily account and contract histories, Intents-flow records and a long historical block archive remain unavailable. Protocol TVL monitoring covers only up to eight selected histories and a filtered provider index, not every dApp; pool coverage is limited to GeckoTerminal's first page. DEX turnover is not a net flow. DefiLlama fee history is provider-indexed and is not a receipt-level fee ledger or revenue series. Stablecoin peg snapshots lack provider price timestamps and cannot prove redemption or reserves; aggregate stablecoin supply can change with provider coverage, migration, reclassification or historical corrections and does not prove withdrawals or solvency stress. No universal keyless NEAR APR/APY endpoint is connected, so the app does not fabricate a yield alert. Browser observations retain the raw finalized block and only the matched-chunk fields and transaction hashes needed to replay sampled counts; full transaction bodies and receipt payloads are discarded. Missing indicators stay unavailable rather than being displayed as placeholders. See [track alignment](docs/track-alignment.md), [methodology](docs/methodology.md), the [data-source inventory](docs/data-sources.md) and [risk research](docs/risk-radar-research/README.md). No hackathon submission or public post was made.
